ABOUT US

Entertainment, with curiosity.

ScreenPulse is an independent entertainment publication for readers who enjoy looking a little closer.

What we publish

We create original explainers, features, and practical guides about movies, anime, gaming, streaming culture, and the ideas behind the entertainment people enjoy.

Our editorial approach

We aim to be clear, useful, and respectful of readers. Our articles should offer context or analysis—not simply repeat headlines. We work to distinguish opinion from fact and correct meaningful errors when they are brought to our attention.
