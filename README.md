# ScreenPulse — AdSense-ready starter website

A responsive static entertainment website starter with article pages, ad placeholders, About, Contact, Privacy Policy, Terms, `robots.txt`, `sitemap.xml`, and an `ads.txt` placeholder.

## Before publishing
1. Buy a domain from a reputable domain registrar.
2. Replace every `YOUR-DOMAIN.example` with your real HTTPS domain in canonical links, `robots.txt`, and `sitemap.xml`.
3. Replace `YOUR-EMAIL@example.com` with a working email address.
4. Edit About, Privacy, and Terms so they truthfully describe the real publisher, services, cookies, analytics, and advertising.
5. Publish several more genuinely useful, original articles. Review every sample article and make it your own; don't rely on a few starter articles as the whole site.
6. Test all links on desktop and mobile. Set up HTTPS and submit the sitemap in Google Search Console.

## Deploying with GitHub Pages (free hosting; custom domain supported)
1. Create/sign in to a GitHub account and create a new repository.
2. Upload the contents of this folder (upload `index.html`, `assets/`, `articles/`, and other files at the repository root—not the containing folder).
3. In repository Settings → Pages, publish from the main branch/root.
4. Add your custom domain in Pages settings and configure the DNS records that GitHub specifies for your domain. Wait for DNS to propagate, then enable HTTPS.
5. Visit your domain and verify all pages, mobile layout, and HTTPS work.

Other static hosts can work too; follow their official custom-domain and HTTPS instructions.

## Google AdSense setup
1. Review the current eligibility and program policies in the official AdSense Help Center.
2. Apply using the Google account you control and enter your real website URL and publisher details.
3. Follow the review steps shown in AdSense. Add the site verification code exactly as instructed by Google, usually in the `<head>` of the pages or through the platform integration.
4. Wait for approval. Approval is not guaranteed and can depend on content quality, site navigation, policy compliance, and eligibility.
5. Only after approval, copy the exact ad code from your AdSense account and place it where you want ads to appear. The gray dashed boxes in the template are visual placeholders; they do not display real ads.
6. If AdSense provides an `ads.txt` record, copy the exact record to the root `ads.txt` file. Do not guess your publisher ID.
7. If you have visitors in regions requiring consent, configure an appropriate consent management platform and privacy disclosures before serving ads.
8. Never click your own ads, ask others to click them, buy fake traffic, or place ads where accidental clicks are likely.

## Important
This template does not itself connect to AdSense or guarantee approval/earnings. Google must review and approve the live site. Privacy and legal pages are starting templates, not legal advice. Use only content and images you have rights to publish.
